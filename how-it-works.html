GYEP / HOW IT WORKS

FROM IDEA
TO OBJECT.

Choose a product, send the details, approve the design, then let us handle the print and delivery.

THE PROCESS

FIVE STEPS.
NO MYSTERY.

For custom products, production starts after the design has been approved.

0101

Choose

Pick the product you want from the GYEP catalog.

0202

Personalize

Send the photo, name, link, size, style, or other details required by the product.

0303

Approve

Review the design or production preview. Once approved, the production clock begins.

0404

Print

The selected production option is used to schedule the job. Sunday is a production holiday.

0505

Deliver

After quality checking and packing, the finished piece is shipped by GYEP.

Important: production time can vary with queue conditions and the number of figures/orders being produced.
