GYEP / ABOUT

THINGS
MADE PERSONAL.

GYEP is a 3D printed product brand for people who want to turn photos, names, places, links, and ideas into something physical.

THE IDEA

START WITH
SOMETHING YOU MEAN.

A photo can become a lithophane. A person can become a figurine. A name can become an object. A place can become a keepsake. A digital link can become something people can scan from a desk or counter.

That is the space GYEP plays in: personal objects made with 3D printing, presented in a simple way, and ordered without an unnecessarily complicated process.

YOUR IDEA DOESN'T HAVE TO STAY ON A SCREEN.
WHAT WE CARE ABOUT

SIMPLE.
PERSONAL. USEFUL.

Three principles shape how the catalog and ordering experience is built.

01

Personal

Products start from something specific to you: a person, photo, name, place, or idea.

02

Clear

Choices, production timing, and ordering steps are written to be easy to understand before you commit.

03

Practical

Alongside keepsakes, the catalog includes useful pieces for desks, homes, events, and everyday use.

THE BRAND

GYEP IS
THE MASTER BRAND.

The catalog can grow across different kinds of products without being locked into the word “Store”. The visual system stays simple, while each collection can have its own character.

At the center is one idea: make something people can keep, use, display, or give — and make the journey to order it feel straightforward.
